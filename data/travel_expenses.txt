NEXUS CORP TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Booking Flights, Rail, and Hotel Accommodations
All domestic and international business travel must be arranged at least 14 days prior to departure through the corporate travel management portal (Navan). For domestic flights under 6 hours, standard economy class is required. Business class booking is permitted for continuous international flights exceeding 8 total hours. Hotel accommodations must not exceed $250 per night in standard metropolitan areas or $350 per night in designated high-cost cities like New York, London, and Tokyo.

2. Daily Food Allowances (Per Diem) and Client Meals
Employees traveling on official company business receive a flat daily meal allowance (per diem) of $80 per day. Itemized receipts are mandatory for any individual food or beverage expense that exceeds $25. Alcoholic beverages are not eligible for reimbursement under standard meal allowances unless part of an authorized client entertainment event pre-approved in writing by a Vice President.

3. Ground Transportation and Mileage Reimbursement
Travelers are encouraged to use public transit or rideshare services (Uber/Lyft) when traveling to and from airports and client locations. Premium or luxury car rides are strictly prohibited. Personal vehicle usage for business trips is reimbursed at the standard IRS mileage rate, provided the employee submits start and end addresses along with toll receipts.

4. Expense Reimbursement Submissions and Approval Workflows
Expense reports must be filed within 30 calendar days of completing a trip using the Expensify platform. Digital copies or clean JPEG photo scans of itemized receipts must be attached to every claimed item. Expense reports submitted after 45 days will be automatically rejected by the finance department and will not be eligible for reimbursement. Approved expenses are paid out via direct deposit every second Friday.

5. Corporate Credit Card Usage Rules
Executive team members and frequent travelers who hold corporate credit cards must submit monthly reconciliation statements by the 5th of each month. Personal expenses made on corporate credit cards, even if accidental, must be reported to finance immediately and reimbursed within 10 business days.
