NEXUS CORP INTERNAL WORKPLACE & HUMAN RESOURCES POLICIES

1. Flexible Working Hours and Core Schedules
Nexus Corp operates on a flexible scheduling policy designed to accommodate diverse working preferences while maintaining strong team collaboration. Core collaboration hours across all time zones are established from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST. During these core hours, all full-time employees are expected to be available on Slack and respond to critical team inquiries within a 30-minute window. Outside of core hours, employees are free to structure their working days as they see fit, provided they complete 40 hours per week and attend mandatory synchronous group meetings.

2. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policies
All full-time employees are granted 22 days of paid time off per calendar year. PTO accrues on a pro-rata basis at the rate of 1.83 days for every fully worked calendar month. Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for short absences (1-2 days) and 21 days in advance for extended leave (3 or more consecutive business days). A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be carried over into the following calendar year, and these carried-over days expire on March 31st if unused.

3. Parental and Caregiver Leave
Nexus Corp provides 14 weeks of fully paid parental leave for primary caregivers following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Employees must give HR at least 30 days written notice before starting parental leave. Leave must be taken within the first 12 months of the child's arrival.

4. Wellness Allowance and Home Office Setup
To support remote employees, Nexus Corp offers a $100 monthly stipend usable for high-speed home internet, mobile phone plans, or co-working space day passes. Additionally, newly hired full-time employees receive a one-time $750 ergonomic allowance to purchase desks, office chairs, external monitors, or peripheral equipment. Receipts must be submitted to the HR portal within 60 days of hire.

5. Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy
Nexus Corp is committed to providing a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone. Discriminatory behavior, harassment, or intimidation based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or religion is strictly prohibited. Violations should be reported immediately to hr-ethics@nexuscorp.com. Reports are investigated within 5 business days, and non-compliance may lead to immediate termination.
